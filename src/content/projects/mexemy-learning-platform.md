As Founding Full-Stack Engineer at Mexemy, I developed and maintained the production web platform and internal automation used by an education business serving approximately 3,000–5,000 students.

## Building the platform

I developed and maintained the company's production website and internal tools, while balancing technical priorities with the needs of a growing education business.

## Automating community operations

I integrated Discord automation for the private student community. This reduced manual work around learner onboarding, access management, and community operations.

I also built internal tools to automate repetitive operational work.

## Leading beyond implementation

I built and led the company team, including the development team, and managed day-to-day business operations. The role combined hands-on software engineering with ownership of technical and operational priorities as the business grew to BDT 9M in annual revenue in 2024.

## My contribution

My work covered the production platform, internal automation, community integrations, and team leadership throughout my time at Mexemy.
