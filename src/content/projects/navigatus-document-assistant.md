At Navigatus, I developed an internal retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) assistant for proprietary operational documents, helping staff query the organization's own material.

## Bringing documents into the assistant

I implemented document ingestion so operational documents could become part of the assistant's information source. The assistant focuses on internal material used by staff in their work.

## Supporting staff queries

I implemented conversational context for staff queries, supporting interactions that continue beyond a single question.

## My contribution

I developed the internal RAG assistant, including document ingestion and conversational context, as part of my software engineering role at Navigatus. The work sits alongside my broader focus on AI document processing and operational systems.
